[[bert|BERT]] "was originally implemented in the English language at two model
sizes, BERT-BASE (110 million parameters) and BERT-LARGE (340 million parameters)",
both trained on the Toronto BookCorpus (800M words) and English Wikipedia (2,500M
words), with weights released on GitHub (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-bert.md.extracted.md).

[[wikipedia-bert|On 11 March 2020, 24 smaller models were released]], the smallest being BERT-TINY at
4 million parameters (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-bert.md.extracted.md).
