"The term retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) was introduced in a 2020 paper that
described combining a parametric language model with a non-parametric external
[[wikipedia-rag|memory accessed through retrieval at inference time]]" (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-rag.md.extracted.md). The
technique "was first proposed in 2020 and has since become a widely adopted approach
in modern AI systems" (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-rag.md.extracted.md).
