[[wikipedia-deep-learning|Machine learning on multilayered neural networks]], for classification, regression
and representation learning. "Deep" = many layers, from three to thousands
(vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-deep-learning.md.extracted.md). Methods may be supervised, semi-supervised or
unsupervised.

Common architectures: fully connected nets, deep belief networks, recurrent nets,
[[convolutional-neural-network|CNNs]], GANs, [[transformer-architecture|transformers]],
neural radiance fields (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-deep-learning.md.extracted.md). Applied across computer
vision, speech recognition, NLP, machine translation, bioinformatics, drug design,
medical imaging, climate science and board games — in some cases surpassing human
expert performance.

Early neural nets took inspiration from information processing in biological
systems, particularly the brain. Current networks do not aim to model brain
function and are considered low-quality models for that purpose
(vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-deep-learning.md.extracted.md).
