[[wikipedia-prompt-engineering|Structuring natural-language inputs to produce specified outputs]] from a generative
model (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-prompt-engineering.md.extracted.md). Context engineering is the adjacent
software-engineering area covering the non-prompt context — system instructions,
metadata, API tools and tokens.

Effective practice means understanding how a model interprets language, and covers
few-shot prompting, [[chain-of-thought-prompting|chain-of-thought prompting]] and
role assignment (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-prompt-engineering.md.extracted.md). Common techniques: multi-shot,
chain-of-thought and [[tree-of-thoughts|tree-of-thought]] prompting, plus assigning
roles. Automated prompt generation such as
[[retrieval-augmented-generation|RAG]] widens accuracy and scope
(vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-prompt-engineering.md.extracted.md).

During the 2020s AI boom prompt engineering became a business capability and
"prompt engineer" a job title; the title has since lost traction as models produce
better prompts than humans and corporate prompting training generalised
(vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-prompt-engineering.md.extracted.md).

**Prompt injection** is a cybersecurity attack targeting ML models through
malicious prompts (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-prompt-engineering.md.extracted.md).
