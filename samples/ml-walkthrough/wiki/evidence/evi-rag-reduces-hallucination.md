**Claim.** Retrieving documents before generating reduces fabrication and makes
answers checkable.

**Mechanism.** Rather than relying on static training data, RAG pulls relevant text
from databases, uploaded documents or web sources, and the model consults that set
before responding (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-rag.md.extracted.md).

**Reported effect.** The method "helps reduce AI hallucinations, which have caused
chatbots to describe policies that don't exist, or recommend nonexistent legal
cases to lawyers that are looking for citations to support their arguments"
(vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-rag.md.extracted.md).

**Secondary effects.** Less retraining on new data, saving computational and
financial costs; and sources included in responses, so users can verify and
cross-check retrieved content (vault:20260728-231916-local-wiki-rag.md.extracted.md).

**Status.** The source is [[wikipedia-rag|an encyclopedia summary reporting adoption]] and claimed
benefits, not a controlled measurement. Treat effect sizes as unquantified here.
